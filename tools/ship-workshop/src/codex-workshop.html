Rotation rules

Each ship has a seven-day window. Annual ships return on the same calendar date. Occasional ships skip years, or appear once only.

Multiple ships can overlap. A window starts at 00:00 UTC and closes exactly 168 hours later.

New families start as Common seeds. Refined forms return annually, Advanced forms every two years, and final Legendary forms every five years. Previously saved assignments remain editable.

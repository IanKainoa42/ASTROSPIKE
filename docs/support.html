ASTROSPIKE Support

Getting started

ASTROSPIKE is zero-gravity volleyball. Fly your ship, spike the ball into your opponent's goal, and switch sides between sets. Play solo against the AI, pass-and-play on one device, or duel a friend online through Game Center.

Controls

  • Steer: Tap and hold either side of the screen to turn toward it
  • Thrust: Your ship accelerates forward automatically toward where it's pointed
  • Spike: Fly into the ball nose-first to send it flying down your aim line
  • Tractor beam: Hold to pull the ball toward you from a distance

Tips

  • Hulls: Pick a ship in the Hangar. Hulls are cosmetic only — every hull handles identically
  • Sets: Matches are best-of-3 or best-of-5 sets; teams swap sides between sets
  • Online play: Invite a friend through Game Center from the main menu

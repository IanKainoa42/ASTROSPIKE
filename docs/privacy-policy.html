ASTROSPIKE Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version

ASTROSPIKE does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What the app stores locally

  • Your hull selection and hangar unlocks
  • Onboarding/tutorial completion state
  • Control and gameplay settings

This data is stored using Apple's standard on-device preferences (UserDefaults) and never leaves your device.

Game Center

ASTROSPIKE uses Apple's Game Center for friend invites and multiplayer matchmaking. Game Center is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's own privacy policy. ASTROSPIKE does not receive or store your Game Center data beyond what's needed to run a match.

Multiplayer

When you play online, match data (ship positions, ball state, score) is relayed between players to keep the match in sync. This data is not stored after the match ends.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. ASTROSPIKE never sees or stores your payment information.

## Description (4000 char max)

```
Fly a momentum-driven lander in a sealed arena and spike a luminous ball through a hanging net — the only goal in the game.

HOW IT WORKS
Two ships face off across a single net suspended from the roof. The ball is fast, elastic, and never stops bouncing. Thrust into it nose-first to send it screaming toward your opponent's side. If it enters the net from their face, you score. First to 7 wins with a two-point lead; 11 is the hard cap.

CONTROLS
Hold either side of the screen to steer toward it. Your ship thrusts forward automatically along its nose. Gravity pulls you down at all times. Momentum is everything — every turn, every boost, every collision changes your trajectory. There are no brakes.

PHYSICS THAT MATTER
Linear momentum persists. Rotation stops the instant you let go. The ball rebounds off walls, the floor, the ceiling, the net cap, and both ships. A ball sitting on the far lip rolls into the goal. The roof bulges over the net so ceiling rides don't gift easy points. Ships pass freely through the net but get pushed back the deeper they cross into the opponent's half.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Solo — Race an AI that flies under the same constraints you do. It reads the ball, picks a setup, and drives through contact. Difficulty changes reaction time and aim, never the physics.
• Pass & Play — Hand the device to a friend. Same arena, same rules, two thumbs each.
• Online Duels — Game Center matchmaking. Real-time relay keeps both boards in sync. Invite a friend or find a stranger.

COSMETIC HULLS
Four premium hulls are available in the Hangar: Bulwark, Wraith, Hornet, and Comet. They change how your ship looks — nothing else. Flight, thrust, collision, and scoring are identical for every hull, in every mode.

FREE FOREVER
Every mode, every rule, every match is free. No ads, no energy bars, no pay-to-win. The only purchases are the four cosmetic hulls.

REQUIREMENTS
• iOS 18.0 or later
• iPhone or iPad
• Game Center sign-in required for online duels
• iCloud sign-in required for lobby presence
```

## App Review Notes

```
ASTROSPIKE is a free-to-play zero-gravity volleyball game. All modes and rules are free.

IN-APP PURCHASES: Four cosmetic hull skins ($0.99 each). They change the ship's appearance only — flight, thrust, collision, and scoring are identical for every hull. To test: main menu → HANGAR → tap a locked hull tile → buy. Restore Purchases is in the same screen.

GAME CENTER: Online duels require Game Center sign-in. Two devices with Game Center accounts can match via invitation or auto-match.

ICLOUD/CLOUDKIT: The lobby uses CloudKit to show who is online. Presence records auto-expire. No user-generated content is stored beyond display name, hull selection, and match results.

NO TRACKING: The app does not use ATT, IDFA, or any third-party analytics. NSPrivacyTracking is false.

DEMO ACCOUNT: Not applicable — no login system. The app uses Game Center authentication only.
```
